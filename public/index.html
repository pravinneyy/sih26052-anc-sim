    Solution Architecture

    Dual-lane hybrid tactical architecture partitioning hard real-time 48 kHz active noise cancellation (<90 µs latency budget) on Core 0 and 16 kHz causal subband speech enhancement (12 ms latency budget) on Core 1.

    Interactive Dual-Lane Signal Flow

    Analog Latency Budget Breakdown

    48 kHz Active Cancellation Path 88.5 µs / 90.0 µs
    16 kHz Speech Enhancement Path 12.0 ms / 15.0 ms

    Sound propagates across 3.0 cm of earcup airgap in ~88 µs. Our Cortex-M7 Core 0 executes the entire ADC → DMA → FxLMS filter → DAC cycle in <88.5 µs, guaranteeing zero acoustic phase distortion.

    What to demonstrate

    Load the impulsive clip and process it. Then turn the two switches off one at a time and process again — they do different jobs, and the measurements below say so.

    Impulse attenuation is the loud win. You hear it immediately.

    Freezing the noise estimate is the subtle one. It gives up a little raw suppression to keep more speech in the half-second after each impulse — the words that would otherwise be eaten. Intelligibility, not loudness. Same trade-off shape as the finding on the cancellation path.
