    Live Audio Processing Lab

    Speak into the mic — or play a file or built-in clip through the same path — and the background noise is stripped out live while your voice comes through, audible within a couple of chunks. The trained SIH26052 CRN/GRU model runs alongside a classical noise tracker that adapts to whatever your microphone actually hears. This is the single-channel half of the architecture — the half that runs on the boom microphone and feeds the radio.

    Use headphones for the microphone source — enhanced audio plays back immediately, and speakers next to an open mic will feed back.

    What to demonstrate

    Start live enhancement on the mic, stay quiet for a second, then talk with a fan, AC or traffic noise in the background. Flip Raw/Enhanced back and forth without stopping — on Enhanced the background drops away between words while your voice stays clear.

    Clap, knock or drop something: the Impulses blocked counter ticks up and the bang is ducked instead of reaching the listener.

    Drag Noise suppression down to 15 dB for a more natural room tone, or up to 50 dB for near-total silence between words.

    Signal chain

    Rate16 kHz, mono
    Frame512 samples, 32 ms
    Hop128 samples, 8 ms, 75% overlap
    WindowHann, WOLA (matches training's torch.stft/istft)
    ModelCausal CRN (3 conv layers) + 1-layer GRU (96 units)
    Streaming chunk8 new frames (64 ms) per inference call
    Recurrent stateGRU hidden state persisted across chunks
    Noise trackingMCRA minimum statistics (1.5 s) + fast band floor for swelling noise
    Gain ruleOM-LSA with speech-presence probability, fused with the neural mask
    Voice gateCloses between words (240 ms hold), low cut below 90 Hz
    Impulse handlingEnergy-ratio onset + broadband check / neural impulse head, ducked 30 dB

    Method notes

    The model, training recipe and offline batch path are documented in sih_python/CRN_GRU_AUDIO_ML.md. This live engine runs the same trained checkpoint, re-exported with the GRU hidden state exposed (sih_python/export_streaming_onnx.py) so the browser can call it one small chunk at a time instead of only on a whole pre-recorded clip — see that script's docstring for exactly how the hidden state and the causal convolutions' receptive field are carried across chunks.
